Important: $90.3 million is a conditional projection, not guaranteed revenue starting in 2029. It depends on project completion, actual taxable valuations, the law in effect, and an applicable excess levy. It should not be multiplied by five or ten years without an independently supported commencement schedule.

If renewed

Existing excess levy authority continues for the five fiscal years beginning July 1, 2029. Under the current framework, Mason County Schools can receive qualifying excess levy revenue from completed data-center projects, subject to actual assessments and collections.

If not renewed

The current excess levy expires after its authorized term. Under the current legal framework, without an applicable school excess levy, the school system would not receive the projected data-center excess levy revenue. Future legislation or a subsequent levy could change this result.

The treasurer's October 13, 2026 report estimates $90.3M per year for schools from the Nscale Monarch and Starwood Tamarack projects when completed. The renewal ballot separately estimates approximately $9.923M annually for listed school purposes. These figures have different bases and should not be presented as guaranteed simultaneous receipts.
